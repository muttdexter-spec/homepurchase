# QA pass, 12 September 2026: every listing photo-graded

## What was wrong

The handoff said 24 listings were scored and 11 were done "in detail". The photo evidence was thinner than that:

- **11 listings had no photo pass at all** (Ravine, Cavendish, Bunton, Seton-era batch: Crosby, Lark, Mountain Grove, Rexway, Centennial, Pallatine, Pondview, Stillmeadow). Their costs were pure era priors, and three of them (Ravine, Cavendish, Bunton) sat at ranks 3, 4 and 6 of the shortlist on nothing.
- **Three "detailed" listings were mostly unread.** Tania, Pinemeadow and Bonnie each had 11 of 15 condition tells at `not_shown` despite 43 to 50 photos. Delaney had 6 unread, Clinton 7.
- **13 listings on the second saved search had never been entered into v3** at all: Maplewood, Osborne, Columbus, Weir, Wood, Seton, Middlesmoor, Christina, Oxlow, Cherrywood, Kent, Barberry, Samford.
- Mountain Grove was in the model under an ITSO listing ID that has since dropped; the live TRREB copy (W13767884) replaces it. The Appleby STOP now carries its address, 5322 Windermere Drive.

## What was done

**Stage 1** on the 13 new listings (crawler extended to keep remarks, room table, zoning, facing, badge).

**Stage 2 photo pass on all 24 unread listings, and a re-read of the 5 thin ones.** 29 passes, roughly 4 screenshots each, at an 8-frame-per-screenshot layout that reads eight 2.4x crops at once. Every listing now has 11 to 14 of 15 tells settled; the only fields still unread anywhere are ones the photo sets do not contain (panels, tubs behind curtains, a few sinks hidden behind islands).

**Model v3.1** (`score.py`, `costs.yaml`), four changes, each documented in the code:

1. `eff_year()`: build year falls back to a neighbourhood estimate (`year_built_est`, source noted per record) and then to the TRREB age band at its older end. The README claimed this fix existed; it did not. Nine listings were being priced at the pre-1980 prior and 25% contingency because MLS left the year blank.
2. `below_grade()`: when MLS gives no basement area but says finished, `usable()` counted zero while `cost()` charged 45% of above-grade as basement to finish. Both now use 70% of the footprint (above-grade / storeys), calibrated on the 20 listings that do state an area (median stated/estimate = 1.11, so the estimate is conservative). This is the change that moves bungalows most: Enfield goes from #11 to #3, Columbus enters at #6. The `bg_sqft_est` column in `decision.csv` says which rows rely on it, and `below_grade_estimate_factor: 0` in `costs.yaml` turns it off.
3. Kitchen: a retained bulkhead over visibly new boxes (`kitchen_boxes_new`) no longer triggers a full-gut line (Barberry, Maplewood). A second full bath confirmed original (`secondary_bath_original`) is priced at p=1.0 (Tania, Weir). New enum values: `laminate_dropin` (defect), `subway_modern`, `integrated_modern`, `vessel_on_wood`, `raised_panel_square` (not defects).
4. Reserve: a metal roof (MLS or photo) is charged at 30% (Middlesmoor). Verdict notes now flag in-ground pools, tenancy and virtual staging without changing the verdict class, except that virtual staging drops a SEE FIRST to SEE (Tania).

`decision.csv` gained six columns: `year_used`, `year_src`, `bg_sqft_est`, `tells_seen_of_15`, `photos_total`, `stage2_date`.

**Process fixes** in `SKILL.md` and `stage2_brief.md`: the $/sq ft gate now bands by size and exempts A/A− (the defect the README flagged); the photo protocol is rewritten around the 800×1300 viewport and the `__fit` / `__read` 8-frame helpers, which cut a pass from about 12 tool calls to 6.

**Deliverables regenerated** by a new `build_walkthrough.py` (the page was hand-built before; it now rebuilds from the model outputs in one command, all 37 cards, same design). The artifact *Walking the Shortlist* is republished in place. `renovation-cost-detail.md` covers all 37.

## Second pass the same night (v3.2)

- **Rank band** (`rank_low`, `rank_high`; on the card rail as "rank if unknowns flip"): where the house lands if the basement estimate is switched off, unread windows turn out original, the kitchen is a gut, or the floors need replacing. Enfield reads 3 to 17: its #3 is the basement estimate and nothing else. Delaney 2 to 2, Tania 1 to 1, Columbus 6 to 11, Middlesmoor 6 to 14.
- **Evidence grade** (`evidence`, `evidence_notes`; a stamp on every card): high when 12+ tells settled and at most one estimated input; medium or low otherwise, with the reason (virtual staging, estimated year, estimated basement, no basement frame, under 25 photos). Tania, Pinemeadow, Cavendish, Rexway, Grand, Centennial, Osborne, Crosby, Samford, Wyandotte are medium; nothing is low.
- **Cash to close at 20% down** in the CSV, same arithmetic as the page calculator.
- **Effective bedrooms**: a bedroom under 60 sq ft in the room table no longer earns layout points. Middlesmoor's 8.3 x 5.0 ft "bedroom" drops it from C to C−.
- **HDR-severe rule is now code**: `hdr_blowout: severe` voids every finish read before scoring, instead of relying on the person doing the pass to remember.
- **Duplicate detection**: two records with the same street address (the Mountain Grove ITSO/TRREB case) now print a warning at scoring time.
- **Sensitivity flips made real**: the old "agent says roof+HVAC recent" flip touched a field the model never read; replaced with the basement-estimate and original-windows flips.
- `refresh.sh` runs score, detail export, agent questions, the regression test (`test_model.py`, 8 invariants per listing) and the page build in one command.
- `deliverables/agent-questions.md`: the two standing questions plus house-specific ones for all 37, generated from the records.

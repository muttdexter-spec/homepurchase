## 2. The error I made, and the correction

When Alex was asked for his payment numbers he was told the batch ran $5,211 to $7,201 a month.
That figure was `own_mo` from `decision.csv`, which is the five-year cost of ownership divided by
sixty months. It is **not** P&I + tax + upkeep, which is what the question asked about and what
`price_component()` now uses.

The true monthly payment at the `costs.yaml` defaults runs **$4,140 to $6,421**. Cross-check: the
OneHome portal estimates $6,363/mo for Woodvale against $6,403 computed here.

Consequences of the wrong frame:

- **24 of 39 houses sit at price 100.** Among those, price contributes nothing and the other seven
  components decide. This is the consequence V6.1 §3 predicted, at a larger scale than intended.
- **The $7,000 maximum gates nothing.** No house in the batch reaches it, so the soft-ceiling
  refinement, though implemented and correct, does not fire on this batch.

If price should separate this batch, comfortable near $5,300 and maximum near $6,400 would do it.
That is a two-line change to `costs.yaml` `price:` and a rebuild. **Alex's stated numbers are what
is in the file; nothing was changed on his behalf.**

### Caveats, each verified rather than assumed

1. **Through Burlington the QEW and ON-403 are the same roadway** and OSM tags those ways with both
   refs, so `qew_m` and `h403_m` are equal for most houses. Quiet takes the minimum over all four
   line features, so the double count changes no score, but the two columns are not independent.
2. **Lake Ontario relation 1206310 includes Burlington Bay.** For Cherrywood (473 m), Crosby
   (686 m), Lark and Enfield the nearest water is the bay, not the open lake. Kept deliberately,
   because LaSalle Park and Spencer Smith are the waterfront those houses walk to. The card says
   "to the water", not "to the lake", for that reason.
3. **The assigned-school lookup was NOT done.** The HDSB and HCDSB locators are ArcGIS apps and the
   Fraser ratings are a second source. The school part scores the distance half from
   `nearest_school_km` and the rating half at its midpoint, which is why most houses read 11.2 of
   15. `location.assigned_school_done: false` marks this. One line in the mast, not 37 card stamps.
4. **Woodvale and Duncaster carry two estimated distances each.** The Overpass mirror would not
   serve the rail and lake queries again, so `rail_m` and `lake_m` for those two are carried from
   the nearest already-measured house (Aldridge and Centennial for Woodvale, Middlesmoor 200 m away
   for Duncaster). Both estimates sit far outside their thresholds, so neither changes a point of
   either score. Each `geo.json` entry says so in its own `_note`. Replace on the next clean pull.

## 4. Ensuite coverage is worse than the spec assumed

V6.1 §5 expected room tables to settle 25 houses. `observations.json` carries a room table for only
16. So `ensuite_state()` reads the table where there is one, and `ensuite_with_basis()` falls back
to the bath count where there is not: one full bath means no ensuite, three or more full baths on
three or more bedrooms means there is one. The card records which of the two decided it.

Result: **11 yes, 7 no, 21 unknown.** The 21 carry `Layout partly estimated`. The ensuite term is
therefore doing less work than intended. Closing it needs either the room tables pulled from the
remaining listings or an agent question.

## 5. The ensuite decision was the assistant's, not the buyer's

Asked whether a primary ensuite matters, Alex answered "unsure, do what you think is best".
`ensuite_matters: true` is the assistant's choice. `buyer_answers.note_ensuite` says so in those
words. **Do not restate this as an elicited preference.** Reasoning on file: the flaw it fixes is
real, and the weight on layout comes from the pairs either way, so the cost of being wrong is
bounded.

Likewise `buyer_answers.note_space`: his answer carried three numbers ("cramped below 1300, small
at 1400, enough at 1700") and the ramp takes two. 1300 and 1700 are his first and third. The middle
number has no slot in a straight ramp and is recorded rather than dropped. If the ramp is ever made
piecewise, 1400 is the bend.

- **The basement rec room is under a drop-tile grid ceiling.** The lower level was refreshed, not

- **Nothing mechanical appears in 44 photos.** No furnace, no panel, no water heater, no utility
  room, despite remarks claiming a 2021 panel and a 2023 furnace.

- MLS Garage Spaces 0 contradicts Attached Garage: Yes and "Built In, Garage". Recorded as 0, the
  conservative read, matching the Mountain Grove carport precedent. **If there is a real built-in
  garage, parking goes 50 -> 100 and the house climbs. Ask the agent.**

The photo pass moved it from 28th to 21st: condition 45 -> 58, expected work $125,912 -> $96,264.

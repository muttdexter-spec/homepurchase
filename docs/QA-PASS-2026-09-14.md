## 5. What changed in the model

- **One score, eight components, sorted descending.** `dollars_per_fit_point_mo`, `rank_value` and
  `fit_dollars_mo` are gone from code, yaml and csv. Gated rows write `-` everywhere a rank would
  go; nothing writes 91 to 93 any more.
- **`p_hold(age, life, H)`**, used in both condition and the reserve term of the hold cost. A
  confirmed-original window now reaches the money over the hold instead of being annualised over
  thirty years (review 5.7).
- **`work_full` puts every probability at 1, `p_hold` included.** The first cut kept `p_hold` in the
  denominator, where it cancelled on exactly the lines it was added for. Corrected. Condition moved
  on the six houses checked: Crosby +4.9, Lark +4.7, Oxlow +3.8, Christina +3.1, Maplewood +2.9,
  Columbus 0.0. Columbus is the check that the fix is doing what it should: it is the 2008 house
  whose furnace and A/C are original and at end of life, so its `p_hold` was already 1.0.
- **The five `_seen` fields** (`roof_seen`, `furnace_seen`, `ac_seen`, `water_heater_seen`,
  `panel_seen`) with the vocabulary `original` / `replaced_<year>` / `new`, and the four year boxes
  in the showing record now export into them.
- **The pool carry is a priced reserve line**, p = 1, life 1. Ravine, Centennial, Rexway and Oxlow.
- **`condition.photos_may_lower_p`**, default true. With it false, Seton falls from 70.1 to 50.8.
- **G4**, the project gate. Enfield is the only house above 15% of ask in expected work, and it is

- **The band** is nine rankings, not three.
- **`hold.cost_of_capital` is derived**, not a free key. 4.0%, the same number v5 asserted.
- **`overpay` is still computed** and still in `decision.csv`. Its one use on the page is the first

## 9. Everything I was unsure about

1. **Price anchors at other holds.** Section 4 fixes the price anchors at $270,000 and $420,000 over
   five years. At a ten-year hold every house clamps to zero and the band stops measuring anything,
   so I expressed the same anchors per year ($54,000 and $84,000) and scale them with the hold. The
   five-year numbers are identical to the specification; the 3 and 10 year rankings are my reading
   of what the spec meant.
2. **The pool carry in `work_full`.** Its life is 1 and its `p_hold` is `H`, a count of annual
   payments rather than a probability. I left that multiplier on both sides of the condition ratio.
   Treating it as a probability would make five years of pool carry read as five times more work
   than a full gut.
3. **Oxlow's pool field contradicts its own remarks.** The field says `above-ground`, the remarks say
   "in-ground pool (new liner 2025)". Acceptance section B expects Oxlow to carry the pool line, so
   I made the remarks win when they say in-ground and the field does not. Wood and Maplewood are
   untouched. **The field should be corrected at the next stage-2 pass.**
4. **The ridge calibration falls through to lambda 0.** On this fifteen-pair design, at least one
   supported component cannot be moved 10 points by a perfectly consistent respondent at any
   shrinkage. Zero is the least shrinkage available, so it is the right setting, but the fit now
   reports `immovable_components` rather than hiding the fact.
5. **Fifteen binary answers pin down a cone, not a point.** Verified on three synthetic respondents

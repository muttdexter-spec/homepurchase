## 3. Model v3.5: a clean read lowers a line, it never deletes it

The rule the grading handoff already stated ("Nothing gets zeroed on photographic evidence, ever")
is now what `cost()` does. Two places:

- **Vinyl window frames** keep the window line at `observed_floor` (0.15), labelled "vinyl frames
  seen: observed floor, not zero". Before, the line was deleted.
- **A breaker panel on a house built before 1970 or of unknown year** keeps the panel and partial
  rewire lines at the floor. A 60 A breaker, a fuse panel or no read are unchanged.

Why it matters: condition is `1 - expected work / possible work`, and deleting a line whose
probability was below the house's own ratio made the ratio worse. A clean read lowered condition on
ten houses in the 58 (review section 3). Now no single clean read can lower condition and no single
defect read can raise it, on every house, tell by tell: that is test 6.24.

**The card's preview uses the real denominator.** "A showing could move this to X if everything
reads clean" was computed with the possible work held still, which disagreed with what the model
would score after that showing. `_resolved_condition()` now returns what the model would score.

Effect on the 26 houses that were not re-read: on the 20 with vinyl windows, expected work rises by
$579 on most and by up to $3,475 where the frames are past their expected life, the payment picks up
$9 to $10 a month through the reserve, and condition rises, because the kept line adds far more to
possible work than to expected work. The other six (five windows unread, one aluminum) do not move.
No house in the 37 had a panel read, so the panel half of the fix changes nothing today. Highest condition
is 79.2 (Crosby), so "nothing above 80 without a showing" still holds.

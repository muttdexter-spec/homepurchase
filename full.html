Wish list
Kitchen, baths, basement, deck. Your choice, your timing. Deliberately kept out of the ranking.
Grade

One rule behind all of this: photos can only ever count against a house, never for it. A kitchen that looks immaculate in forty photos scores exactly the same as a kitchen with no photos at all. So the green column is only ever "no problem found", and the red column is the real agenda for the visit.
